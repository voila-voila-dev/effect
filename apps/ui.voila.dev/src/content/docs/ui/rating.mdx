```tsx
<Rating.Root value={4.2} count={128} />

<Rating.Input value={value} onChange={setValue} />
```

`Rating` displays and accepts fractions; `Rating.Input` collects a whole number
from the user. `Rating.ReviewItem` lays out a rating with its author and comment.
